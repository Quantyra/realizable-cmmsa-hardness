## Findings and disposition

| Finding | Disposition embodied in the proof |
|---|---|
| Advice conditioning changes the law of V | Bayes likelihood bound, deletion-tail truncation, conditional rank estimate and mixture comparison use the actual posterior. |
| Potential h^4 loss in decoded success | Likelihood factor bounds only bad events; favorable probabilities multiply within the joint advice/question law. |
| Vector advice versus uniform subspaces | Explicit coupling bounds dependence probability by 2^(r-J). |
| Undefined/nontransverse tables | Total tables and explicit joint/conditional transversality charges. |
| Single-threshold maximal extension unsupported | Descending threshold ladder and independent threshold guess; no extension assertion at one arbitrary lowered threshold. |
| Completeness loss and dimension integrality | All m+1 blocks charged; rational denominator restriction and legitimate-tuple padding included. |
| Approximate sampler cannot establish realizability | Simultaneous concentration precedes exceptions; completed power-of-two list has an exact sampler. |
| Rational bit lengths do not ensure polynomial common denominator | Upward integer-weight rounding with budget slack. |
| Ambiguous restriction notation | Restriction is explicitly to W(Q) intersect V. |
| Arbitrarily oversized M or D could invalidate runtime wording | Choose least suitable powers of two. |
| Printed list-theorem advice range too broad for checked ancestry | Use only MZ24 revision 1 Theorem 5.26's 10m/rho range. |
| Earlier realizable hardness and priority | Credit Hirahara and limit the advance to the nearlinear exponent; priority remains uncertain. |

## Limits

The external theorem proofs were not completely reproved or machine-checked.
The inaccessible MZ STOC publisher full text limits comparisons to that
version; the inspected arXiv version and exact HN revision are identified
in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). The result is a fixed-parameter randomized
hardness theorem, with no uniform polynomial exponent for growing L,
linear-factor threshold, one-way-function construction, or P-versus-NP
resolution. No optional deterministic-sampling extension is included.
