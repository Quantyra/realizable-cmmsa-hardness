# Real manuscript thresholds in the rational tagged law

The fixed-table composed tagged score has already been proved equal to the ordered physical tagged score for every fixed center/raw table in `ActualTaggedComposedPhysicalSampler.lean`. Consequently a score above the manuscript real threshold `Δ` implies the physical input `α ≥ Δ/2`; that step does not need another clique loss inside this tagged model. The original manuscript star/clique verifier value is a different, currently unrepresented source value: no Lean theorem transfers that verifier's arbitrary fixed assignment and clique-selection loss to `composedLegalValue` or to the tagged composed score. That is the first source-law handoff still missing.

`ActualTaggedMZRealSurrogate.lean` gives the finite-law bridge for nonintegral exponents. For real decoder exponent `P` and composed-value exponent `Q`, choose `p=floor(P)` and `q=ceil(Q)` when both are nonnegative. It proves `q+6≤p≤J` from `Q+8≤P≤J`, applies the existing every-fixed-table composed score theorem with rational thresholds, and proves `8·2^-P≤8·2^-p` and `2^-q≤2^-Q` after casting to real. Thus one predraw canonical table has a mass of first questions at rational conditional density `8·2^-p`, a threshold at least as strong as real `8S`; no integrality of manuscript exponents is assumed. The extra two bits pay for floor/ceiling rounding, on top of the one-bit margin paying for the positive class collision.

The theorem still requires the explicit tagged geometry/cardinality hypotheses and a score above `Δ` for the same fixed tables. It does not prove the original verifier-to-tagged-law transfer, MZ decoding, outer NO contradiction, or any numerical improvement to the NO exponent.
