## 3. `ActualFiniteAppendExactImageEnergy`

**`per_image_energy_eq`** (fixed E with finrank i, and i ≤ c+s)
- **Empty fibre:** both sums are empty, so 0 = ratio·0. No representative is chosen.
- **Nonempty fibre:** the retained sum is |R|·a², the full sum is |M|·a², with |R| = G(c,i) and |M| = G(c+s,i). Both counts are exact fibre counts. `field_simp` then closes the goal, using the denominator G(c+s,i) > 0 from `hi`.
- **i > c:** G(c,i) is a genuine zero product, from the factor j = c. Truncated natural subtraction only hits factors that are already zero, so it introduces no spurious nonzero value.
- Coefficient constancy is used **only within one image**.

**`rank_i_retained_energy_eq`.** Exact partition of the rank-i matrices by their actual range. The ratio does not depend on E, so `Finset.mul_sum` is legitimate. If i > n there are no image subspaces and both sides are 0.

**`append_rank_projection_energy_eq_frame_ratio`.** For an arbitrary real F that is invariant under all paired inverses:

E_{Mat(n,c)}[(A_s P_i F)²] = (G(c,i)/G(c+s,i)) · E_{Mat(n,c+s)}[(P_i F)²].

- The append operator is the unconditional one.
- Each carrier is normalized by its own size, so there is no stray factor.
- The coefficient filter of `fourierCoeff_rankProjection` and restricted Parseval are applied in the correct direction.

**Boundary cases covered:** zero width (c, s or n = 0), rank 0 (empty product = 1), deficient and zero matrices, i > c, and i > n. The case i > c+s is excluded by hypothesis, as in the contract.

- The fresh bytes are consistent with this: there is no `letI`, `ring` does not follow `field_simp`, and the anonymous proof-valued `let`s are elaboration-only.
