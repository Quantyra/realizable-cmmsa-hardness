## Fixed-frame route and remaining proofs

For one selected `A : SideComplement I copies U`, set `e := actualCoordinateEquiv I copies U A` once. Its definition depends on `A` and its `2J`-dimension proof, not on tables or refresh history. The same `e` can therefore be used for the initial table and every refreshed table. `coordinateCenterTable` and `coordinateLeafTable` transport arbitrary ordinary tables, not just tables induced by global source tables.

To apply the coordinate inverse to a particular ordinary complement density, use the `starCoordinateEquiv` and `starLaw_pushforward_actual` for that fixed `A`. The full-acceptance event equality must be proved directly from restriction naturality. For a center `K` contained in a leaf `L`, the transported center and leaf labels are both precomposed with the induced equivalence on their domains; restricting the transported leaf label from the coordinate leaf to the coordinate center must commute with that inclusion. This yields the exact iff between `StarAccepts` in A.1 and `StarAccepts` in `CoordAmbient J`; combine it with `starLaw_pushforward_actual` and event-mass pushforward to derive exact full `StarDensity` equality. The existing `matchesStar_coordinate_iff` is not that event theorem: it assumes an ambient functional `f` matching the center label on K and every leaf label on each entire L. Full star acceptance only compares each leaf to the center on K. One cannot generally choose such an `f` when refreshed leaf labels disagree on overlaps outside K. `ActualOrdinaryStarMatchingFiber.exists_matching_functional` itself requires `jointlyDirect`, so summing over or specializing a global f would impose an unjustified stronger event. `matchingStarMass_actual_coordinate` preserves only that functional matching event. Do not substitute `actual_implies_ordinary_star_accepts` for this coordinate iff; that theorem is a one-way event inclusion used separately to lower-bound ordinary acceptance.

A decoded pair returned in coordinates can be mapped back using `grassCoordinateEquiv.symm` for its advice subspace and containing subspace.
