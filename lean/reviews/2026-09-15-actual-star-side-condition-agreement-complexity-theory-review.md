## Decision

The frozen increment proves the intended local overlap statement. For two good questions `U` and `U'`, a linear functional `f` on the coordinate space of `U` and a linear functional `g` on the equation span of `U'` agree on

```text
equationSpan(row, U') ∩ coordinateSpace(row, U)
```

provided both functionals take the prescribed right-hand side on every equation generator in their respective questions. The previously certified span-intersection identity reduces that intersection to the span generated by the common rows `U' ∩ U`; linearity then extends generator agreement to the whole intersection.

The actual-source wrapper correctly specializes `row` and `rhs` to `I.support` and `I.rowRhs`, and discharges the generic three-coordinate and pairwise-intersection premises with `I.support_card` and `I.pair_intersection`. It does not construct either functional, prove that a suitable functional exists or is unique, or construct a star label or transport map.

This closes the manuscript's local **RHS-respecting overlap-agreement** obligation. It is a necessary algebraic input to side-condition-preserving gluing and transport, but it does not establish the manuscript's imported unique label transport or any star acceptance/value statement. S3138 and the headline theorem remain open.

## Exact mathematical content

`sideCondition_agree_on_intersection` assumes:

- every row support has cardinality three;
- two distinct row supports intersect in at most one coordinate;
- both `U` and `U'` satisfy `GoodQuestion`;
- `f` has the prescribed RHS value on every equation vector from `U`;
- `g` has the prescribed RHS value on every equation vector from `U'`.

For an element `z` of the intersection, the certified identity

```text
equationSpan(row, U') ∩ coordinateSpace(row, U)
  = equationSpan(row, U' ∩ U)
```

puts its underlying vector in the span of equation vectors for common rows. On each common generator, the `hf` and `hg` assumptions both identify the value with the same `rhs e`. The proof restricts both maps to the common span and uses `LinearMap.eqOn_span` to extend equality from the generators. This is the right algebraic argument, and it also covers the empty-intersection case, where the certified span is zero.

`actual_sideCondition_agree_on_intersection` adds no mathematical assumptions beyond the two good-question premises and the RHS-respecting premises on `f` and `g`. It obtains the incidence hypotheses from the actual occurrence-allocation instance. This is a real specialization to the constructed source representation, rather than a theorem about an unrelated abstract row system.

The theorem is deliberately conditional on `hf` and `hg`. Those premises are force-bearing: deleting either would make arbitrary linear maps agree without justification. Their presence means this module proves compatibility of already constructed RHS-respecting functionals; it does not yet prove that the actual source supplies those functionals.

## Manuscript dependency discharged

The manuscript's star-construction contract says that leaf labels are linear functions satisfying the equation RHS values on `H_U`, and that transport between equivalent leaves is uniquely side-condition-preserving. A gluing or transport construction can preserve both prescribed functionals only if they agree on the overlap of their domains. The reviewed theorem supplies precisely the source-specific overlap fact when the overlap has the form

```text
H_U' ∩ coordinateSpace(U).
```

The result should therefore be recorded in the dependency graph as:

The manuscript currently treats unique side-condition-preserving label transport as part of an imported MZ contract. This increment does not replace that contract. It supplies one exact finite-linear-algebra premise needed for a local formal construction of the transport.

## Remaining force-bearing gaps

1. **Unique RHS functional.** A theorem must construct the unique linear functional

   ```text
   psi_U : equationSpan(I.support, U) -> ZMod 2
   ```

   whose value on each equation vector is `I.rowRhs e`. This requires the equation-vector independence/dimension branch or another proof of consistency and uniqueness. The current `hg` premise assumes the required behavior.

2. **Label construction and extension.** The actual label types on spaces such as `L + H_U` must be defined, their nonemptiness shown, and the RHS functional extended while preserving the prescribed values. The present `f` is supplied as an input on the full coordinate space; no uniform-extension count or law follows.

3. **Gluing and minimal transport.** Agreement on an intersection permits a linear map on a sum, but the corresponding existence, uniqueness, forward/inverse transport, representative independence, and presentation descent theorems are still absent.

4. **Actual clique/star kernel.** The equivalence relation on leaves, equivalence-class sampler, resampling marginal/stationarity statement, center restriction maps, and emitted finite `Star` remain to be constructed and connected to the manuscript's dimensions and side conditions.

5. **Acceptance and completeness.** No theorem here says that transported leaf labels agree with a center label, that an honest global assignment produces accepting answers, or that the original and all resampled equation blocks satisfy the claimed union bound.

6. **Soundness and reduction assembly.** The local decoder, maximal-pair argument, star value bound, HN compilation, finite sampler, polynomial-time seeded map, and final randomized reduction remain separate obligations. The theorem contains no runtime or distributional statement.

## Claim boundary

The defensible claim is:
